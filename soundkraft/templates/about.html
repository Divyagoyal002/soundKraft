While you play, SoundKraft records how you answer: whether you were right, how quickly you responded, and how you touched the screen. Over many sessions this shows whether your performance is staying steady compared with your own usual level.

What SoundKraft is not

SoundKraft does not diagnose anything. Its results are tracking indicators only. A change in scores can have many everyday causes (tiredness, illness, a new device, distractions). If you or your family are concerned about memory or thinking, please talk to a doctor.

Your data

Results are stored on this device's SoundKraft server. They are only shown on the caregiver dashboard if you agreed to share them, and the dashboard is protected by a PIN. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.
